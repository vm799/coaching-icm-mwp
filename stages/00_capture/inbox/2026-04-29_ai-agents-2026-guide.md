### 1. Evolution Timeline 2020-2026 — Enterprise Talk Context-Setter

> "From 2020 to 2022: stateless LLM wrappers. 2023: tool use + memory + early frameworks (LangChain, AutoGen). 2024: graph-based orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI). 2025: MCP standardized tool access, structured memory, autonomy loops made agents production-ready. 2026: coordinated clusters with planner, memory, and verifier roles."

**V's use:** Opens any enterprise or teaching talk on "where we are." Sets urgency without mysticism. Shows the speed of change — each phase was ~12-18 months.

**Connects to:** T1 "AI is 70 years old" (broader history) + Enterprise talks on market timing ("we're at 2026 — the production-readiness inflection").

**V's frame:**
> "2022: chatbots. 2024: orchestration experiments. 2025: MCP standardised tool access. 2026: production agents. The question isn't whether agents are ready. It's whether your context architecture is."
