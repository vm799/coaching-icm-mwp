### 2. Andrew Ng: "AI Jobapalooza" — Counter-Fear Frame ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> "There will be no AI jobpocalypse. I predict the opposite: there will be an AI jobapalooza — a lot more good AI engineering jobs, and I'm also optimistic about the future of the overall job market."

> "Contrary to the predictions of an AI jobpocalypse... the skills needed will change because of AI. That makes this a good time to encourage more people to become proficient in AI."
- Attribution: Andrew Ng, The Batch May 8, 2026 — cite (Ng, DeepLearning.AI)

**Brookings finding (supporting):** Companies that invested in AI hired more workers (2025 Brookings study — cite).

**V's enterprise angle:** Fear is the primary enterprise AI blocker. When board-level resistance is rooted in "AI will replace our people" — Ng's framing + Brookings data + Gallup (65% productivity, not replacement) is the counter-argument package.

**V's frame:**
