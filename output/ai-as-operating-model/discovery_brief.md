## The Core Claim

The enterprise AI conversation has shifted. Not tool adoption. Not pilot projects. Not "AI features." The question in every serious boardroom in 2026 is: what is your AI operating model?

IBM Think 2026, YC Summer 2026 RFS, and Van Clief & McDermott (2026) converge on the same gap from three independent directions:

- **IBM Think:** "AI is no longer a feature; it is infrastructure and the operating model."
- **YC (Blomfield):** "The AI tools exist. The company brain layer does not yet."
- **Van Clief & McDermott:** ICM as architecture for end-to-end interpretable AI workflows.

V's positioning: ICM is the architecture that answers the question every board is asking. V has been building and teaching it for two years before IBM named it at their flagship conference.

## Objection Map

**"We have an AI strategy."**
Enterprise response: Strategy is not operating model. Most AI strategies describe tools and use cases. Operating models describe how work gets done across the organisation. Does your strategy show how intelligence flows from capture to output — with governance, human review, and auditability?

**"We'll wait for the technology to mature."**
Enterprise response: IBM Think 2026 confirmed: the technology is mature. The gap is operating model, not capability. Waiting for better tools while competitors build operating models = falling further behind.

**"Our teams aren't ready."**
Enterprise response: Top-down + bottom-up must happen together. V's teaching stream builds bottom-up literacy. V's enterprise stream gives executives the framework. The two tracks run in parallel, not sequentially.
